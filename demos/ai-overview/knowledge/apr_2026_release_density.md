## Claims

- [fact] April 2026 saw an unusually dense cluster of major releases
  across nearly every large lab: Google's Gemma 4 (Apr 2, open-weight,
  Apache 2.0, up to 31B params), Meta's Llama 4 Scout and Maverick
  (Apr 5, open-weight MoE), Anthropic's Claude Mythos (Apr 7, restricted
  access) and Claude Opus 4.7 (Apr 16), Meta's Muse Spark (Apr 8, Meta's
  first proprietary frontier model), OpenAI's GPT-5.5 and GPT-5.5 Pro
  (Apr 23), and xAI's Grok 4.3 (Apr 30).
- [fact] Gemma 4 shipped in four variants (27B dense, 26B-A4B MoE, and
  edge-optimized E2B/E4B), natively handling text, image, and audio.

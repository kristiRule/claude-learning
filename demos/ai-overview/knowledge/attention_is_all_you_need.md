## Claims

- [fact] Introduced the Transformer architecture, based entirely on
  attention mechanisms, with no recurrence (RNNs) or convolutions.
- [fact] Submitted June 12, 2017; presented at NeurIPS 2017.
- [fact] Demonstrated superior quality on machine translation tasks while
  being more parallelizable and requiring significantly less training
  time than prior recurrent architectures.
- [fact] This architecture became the foundation for essentially all
  major subsequent large language models (BERT, GPT family, Claude,
  Gemini, etc.) — the single most consequential architectural paper for
  the current AI era.

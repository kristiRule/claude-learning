## Claims

- [fact] AI capability is described as accelerating, not plateauing;
  industry produced over 90% of notable frontier models in 2025.
- [fact] Several frontier models now meet or exceed human baselines on
  PhD-level science questions, multimodal reasoning, and competition
  mathematics.
- [fact] On SWE-bench Verified (a coding benchmark), performance rose from
  ~60% to near 100% in a single year.
- [fact] The US–China AI model performance gap has effectively closed:
  US and Chinese models have traded the lead multiple times since early
  2025 (DeepSeek-R1 briefly matched the top US model in Feb 2025; as of
  March 2026 Anthropic's top model led by only 2.7%).
- [fact] Global corporate AI investment reached $581.7B (+130% YoY);
  generative-AI-specific investment surged to $170.9B, nearly 5x YoY.
- [fact] Generative AI reached 53% population adoption within three years
  — faster than the PC or the internet — though adoption pace correlates
  strongly with GDP per capita and varies by country.
- [fact] Inference cost for GPT-3.5-equivalent performance dropped ~280x
  in about two years: from $20 per million tokens (Nov 2022) to $0.07 per
  million tokens (Oct 2024). Hardware costs have declined ~30%/year and
  energy efficiency improved ~40%/year, contributing to the drop.
- [fact] The report includes a dedicated Responsible AI chapter tracking
  AI incidents and public-opinion data (exact incident count and framing
  should be pulled directly from the report before quoting a specific
  number on a slide).

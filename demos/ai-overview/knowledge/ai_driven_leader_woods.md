## Claims

- [opinion] Woods argues AI should function as a "strategic co-pilot" in
  decision-making, not merely an automation tool — the book's central
  reframe.
- [opinion] Proposes a "Four Pillars" framework for AI-driven leadership:
  strategy, execution, human talent, and technology.
- [opinion] Proposes a "CRIT" framework for prompting/working with AI:
  Context, Role, Interview, Task.
- [opinion] Claims AI delivers business value in exactly three ways:
  making people more productive, making operations more efficient, and
  making products/services more valuable.
- [opinion] Core thesis: most leaders misuse AI by treating it as "a
  faster assistant" instead of a genuine thinking partner — the shift
  argued for is "from doing more to thinking better."

## Claims

- [fact] AlexNet won the 2012 ImageNet Large Scale Visual Recognition
  Challenge with a top-5 error rate of 15.3%, a 10.8 percentage point
  improvement over the second-place entry (26.2%) — a gap far larger
  than typical year-over-year improvements at the time.
- [fact] Trained a deep convolutional neural network on the full ImageNet
  dataset (1.2 million images, 1,000 classes).
- [fact] Introduced/popularized key techniques still in wide use: ReLU
  activations, dropout regularization, and multi-GPU training.
- [fact] Widely credited as the moment that triggered the modern deep
  learning boom — proof that scale (data + compute + deep networks)
  could dramatically outperform prior hand-engineered computer vision
  approaches.

## Claims

- [fact] DeepMind proposes a "Levels of AGI" framework classifying systems
  by performance and generality (with autonomy as a related third axis),
  explicitly to give the field a shared vocabulary the way SAE's levels of
  autonomous driving did for self-driving cars.
- [fact] The framework proposes five capability levels above "no AI":
  Emerging, Competent, Expert, Virtuoso, and Superhuman — separating depth
  of performance from breadth of generality rather than using one single
  AGI/not-AGI cutoff.
- [fact] Stated purpose: give researchers, policymakers, and regulators a
  common language for discussing progress, precisely because "AGI" as a
  single yes/no label creates unproductive debate.

## Limitations

This is one proposed framework among several competing ones in the
field, not an adopted industry standard — should be presented as "one
useful way to think about it," not as settled taxonomy.

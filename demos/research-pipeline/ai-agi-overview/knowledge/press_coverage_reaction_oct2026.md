## Claims

- [fact] TechCrunch's headline framing: "Anthropic changes usage policy to
  ban model abuse and election interference." This is the dominant framing
  across secondary coverage — the update presented as Anthropic tightening
  rules.
- [fact — THE FRAMING ERROR WORTH FLAGGING] This framing is **imprecise
  on the election point specifically.** The policy did not newly ban
  election interference in the sense implied — deceptive election content
  was already prohibited and remains so. What actually changed on
  elections was the **removal** of a broader ban (personalized campaign
  targeting), not the addition of a new one. "Ban election interference"
  is technically true of the surviving rule but misleadingly implies the
  update made the election section stricter, when on net it did the
  opposite.
- [fact] At least one secondary source gave October 26 as the publication
  date, conflicting with the correct October 8 date confirmed against
  Anthropic's own post.
- [fact] Coverage of the "abuse toward Claude" clause consistently frames
  it as the most novel/newsworthy element — several outlets lead with
  variations of "don't be mean to Claude," which matches Anthropic's own
  framing of it as a new prohibition, unlike most of the other changes.

## Limitations

- Aggregated from multiple outlets rather than one authoritative press
  source; quality and accuracy vary by outlet.
- Included specifically to document **how the update is being
  misread**, not as a source of new facts. Every factual claim in this
  file is checked against the primary sources elsewhere in this run.

## Relevance

Useful for exactly one thing: showing that the primary-source-first
approach in this run caught something the surrounding coverage didn't.
The "ban election interference" framing is the kind of compressed, mostly-
true-but-misleading headline that spreads because it's simpler than the
actual change (remove a broad rule, keep a narrow one). A deliverable
built from this run can correct that in passing — "despite how this is
being reported, the election section actually got *more* permissive" is a
stronger, more original line than restating the consensus coverage.

## Claims

- [fact] Gartner's 2026 hype cycle places **generative AI** in the Trough
  of Disillusionment — organizations are struggling to move pilots into
  production and are questioning ROI after the initial enthusiasm.
- [fact] Gartner's 2026 hype cycle places **agentic AI** separately, at
  the Peak of Inflated Expectations, heading toward its own Trough of
  Disillusionment — i.e., the two "AI" categories are at different points
  on the curve simultaneously, not one single dot.
- [fact] Per Gartner's 2026 CIO and Technology Executive Survey, only 17%
  of organizations have deployed AI agents to date, while 60%+ expect to
  within the next two years.
- [fact] Gartner predicts more than 40% of agentic AI projects will be
  cancelled by the end of 2027, citing escalating costs, unclear business
  value, and inadequate risk controls.
- [fact] Gartner publishes multiple specialized 2026 hype cycles for
  different AI sub-domains (Agentic AI, Physical AI, AI in Software
  Engineering, AI Governance, AI in IT Operations, AI Services, AI and
  Cloud Platform Services) rather than a single unified "AI" hype cycle —
  worth naming as a fact in itself (the hype cycle has fragmented as the
  field has).

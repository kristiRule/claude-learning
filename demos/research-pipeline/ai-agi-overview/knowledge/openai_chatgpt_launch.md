## Claims

- [fact] OpenAI published "Introducing ChatGPT" on November 30, 2022,
  describing it as a sibling model to InstructGPT, released free as a
  "research preview" to gather user feedback.
- [fact] The original announcement was notably understated (a few short
  paragraphs) relative to the impact that followed.
- [fact] ChatGPT reportedly drew roughly 153,000 visits on day one and
  passed 1 million users within five days — an adoption curve widely
  cited as unprecedented for a consumer software product at the time.

## Limitations

The exact day-one/five-day user numbers circulate widely in secondary
coverage (e.g., retrospective pieces from TechCrunch, Simon Willison) but
should be traced to OpenAI's own confirmed figures if stated precisely on
a slide — some widely-repeated numbers in this space originate from
unofficial estimates rather than OpenAI disclosures.

## Relevance

Primary source for **Section 2 (fast history)** and the **cold open** —
the clean "here's the moment the general public met generative AI"
anchor point, five years after transformers, ten years after AlexNet,
and the natural hinge into "the last 12 months" narrative the talk is
built around.

## Key Quotes

> "We are excited to introduce ChatGPT to get users' feedback and learn
> about its strengths and weaknesses. During the research preview, usage

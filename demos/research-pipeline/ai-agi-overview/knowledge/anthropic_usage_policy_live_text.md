## Claims

Exact quoted language from the current policy, by section:

- [fact] **Abuse of models** (under "Do Not Engage in Cruel, Abusive, or
  Psychologically Harmful Conduct"): prohibits to *"Engage in sustained
  and needless abusive or cruel behavior toward our models."* Separately,
  under "Do Not Abuse Our Platform," bars intentionally bypassing
  guardrails (jailbreaking, prompt injection) without Anthropic's prior
  authorization.
- [fact] **Deceptive campaigns** ("Do Not Engage in Deceptive Campaigns or
  Artificial Activity"): prohibits creating/running fake personas,
  accounts, or outlets to mislead about origin or attribution; concealing
  sponsorship of influence campaigns; seeding AI systems with content that
  misstates independence; building or marketing astroturfing/sockpuppet
  tooling.
- [fact] **Elections** ("Do Not Undermine Democratic Processes"):
  prohibits *"Generate or disseminate false or misleading information in
  political and electoral contexts."* Also bars content falsely appearing
  to come from a real candidate, official, or election authority, and
  encouraging disruption of voting systems or civic processes.
- [fact] **Weapons** ("Do Not Develop or Design Weapons"): prohibits
  *"Develop, produce, modify, design, or test weapons, explosives,
  dangerous materials."* Separately bars designing biological, chemical,
  radiological, or nuclear weapons; modifying agents to increase lethality
  or transmissibility; weaponizing drones/autonomous platforms; illegal
  weapons transfer.
- [fact] **Surveillance** ("Do Not Use for Surveillance, Censorship, or
  Prohibited Law Enforcement or Criminal Justice Purposes"): prohibits
  targeting or tracking a person or their property *"without that
  person's consent."* Covers facial recognition, predictive policing,
  emotion recognition (exempting medical/safety use), doxxing, and
  building surveillance tools. Separately bars using models to make law
  enforcement or criminal justice decisions.
- [fact] **Autonomous hardware** ("High-risk Physical Actions"): applies
  when model outputs drive hardware without human approval. Covered:
  moving through shared space, applying injury-capable force, controlling
  hazardous energy/materials, acting on the human body, controlling safety
  systems, running industrial processes. Requires: *"there must be a
  qualified individual who is able to observe the equipment's operation
  and stop it at any time."* Operating limits must be enforced by the
  equipment or a controller independent of model output.
- [fact] Effective date shown on the page: **November 12, 2026.**

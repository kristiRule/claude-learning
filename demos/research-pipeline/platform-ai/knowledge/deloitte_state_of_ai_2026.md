## Claims

- [fact] **74% of enterprises expect to be using AI agents by 2027.**
- [fact] **Only 21% have mature governance in place.**

## Limitations

- **Obtained via secondary citation, not read from the report.** Both
  figures appear in third-party summaries; the primary PDF was not
  retrieved. Verify both before they go in front of an audience — these are
  the two numbers most likely to be quoted back at the user.
- "Mature governance" is Deloitte's own construct and is not defined in the
  secondary sources. The gap it describes is only as meaningful as that
  definition, which should be read before leaning on it.
- Survey population and methodology unknown from secondary sources.

## Relevance

The **demand-side half of the argument**, and the half an infrastructure
team cannot influence: agents are arriving regardless of whether anyone has
prepared for them.

Its value is that it **rhymes with Gartner independently**. Gartner (via
`gartner_hype_cycle_oct2026` in the AI/AGI run) reports 17% deployed, 60%+
expecting to within two years, and forecasts **>40% of agentic projects
cancelled by end of 2027**. Deloitte reports 74% expecting by 2027 against
21% governance maturity.

Two different research houses, two different instruments, same shape: broad
intent, thin readiness. That convergence is worth more than either figure
alone — and the Gartner cancellation forecast is the plausible consequence
of the Deloitte governance gap. **Whether grounding is the mechanism
connecting them is this run's central open question**, and neither source
establishes it. Do not assert the causal link from these numbers.

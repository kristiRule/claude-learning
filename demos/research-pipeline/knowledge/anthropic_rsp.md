## Claims

- [fact] The Responsible Scaling Policy (RSP) is Anthropic's framework for
  anticipating and addressing risks as models become more capable,
  first released September 2023.
- [fact] Current version is 3.4, effective July 8, 2026 (prior versions:
  3.3 effective May 26, 2026; 3.2 effective Apr 29, 2026; 3.1 effective
  Apr 2, 2026 — the policy is updated frequently).
- [fact] The policy defines AI Safety Level (ASL) thresholds; models
  crossing certain capability thresholds (e.g., related to AI R&D
  automation or CBRN-relevant capability) trigger mandatory additional
  safety evaluations and ASL-3-level security/deployment safeguards
  before deployment.
- [fact] Capability assessments are required on a regular cadence
  (roughly every 6 months per the policy's stated approach).
- [fact] Anthropic states its governance philosophy explicitly: risk
  governance should be "proportional, iterative, and exportable" —
  i.e., intended as a model other labs/regulators could adopt, not a
  one-off internal policy.

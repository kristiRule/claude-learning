## Claims

- [fact] METR proposes the "50%-task-completion time horizon" metric: the
  length of task (measured in human-expert time) that a model can complete
  autonomously with a 50% success rate.
- [fact] This time horizon has grown exponentially over roughly the past
  6 years, with a doubling time of approximately 7 months.
- [fact] At time of publication (March 2025), frontier models such as o3
  had a 50% time horizon of around 110 minutes.
- [fact] Methodology: humans with relevant domain expertise were timed on
  a combination of RE-Bench, HCAST, and 66 novel shorter tasks.
- [fact] Extrapolating the trend, METR projects that within roughly a
  decade AI agents could independently complete a large fraction of
  software tasks that currently take humans days or weeks. (Framed by
  METR as an extrapolation/projection, not a guarantee — keep attributed
  as such.)
- [fact] METR published a follow-up, "Time Horizon 1.1" (Jan 29, 2026,
  https://metr.org/blog/2026-1-29-time-horizon-1-1/) — check this for a
  more current time-horizon figure before finalizing the slide number,
  since the ~110-minute figure is from March 2025.

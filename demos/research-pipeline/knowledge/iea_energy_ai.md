## Claims

- [fact] Electricity demand from data centers grew 17% in 2025; demand
  from AI-focused data centers specifically grew 50% — both far outpacing
  overall global electricity demand growth of 3%.
- [fact] Global data center electricity demand is projected to more than
  double by 2030, to roughly 945 TWh — more than Japan's entire current
  electricity consumption.
- [fact] Tech companies spent over $400B on AI infrastructure in 2025;
  that figure could grow another ~75% in 2026.
- [fact] Electricity use in AI-accelerated servers is projected to grow
  ~30%/year (vs. ~9%/year for conventional servers); AI-focused data
  center power use is on track to roughly triple, approaching that of
  conventional data centers.
- [fact] Despite the fast growth rate, data centers overall are still a
  small share of the total picture today: just over 1% of global
  electricity demand and about 0.5% of global CO2 emissions.
- [fact] In the US and Japan specifically, data centers are expected to
  drive roughly half of electricity demand growth over the next five
  years.

## Limitations

Growth-rate and current-share numbers are both true simultaneously and
should be presented together — citing only the growth rates ("AI data
center demand up 50%!") without the current-share context (~1% of global
electricity) would misrepresent the report's own framing.

## Relevance

Primary source for the environmental angle of **Section 9 (Responsible
AI)**. Pairs directly with the open-weight/small-model efficiency point
in Section 6 — local/small models are a concrete counter-example to

## Claims

- [fact] Three frontier models launched within an 11-day window in
  November 2025: OpenAI's GPT-5.1 (Nov 13), Google's Gemini 3 Pro
  (Nov 18), and Anthropic's Claude Opus 4.5 (Nov 24).
- [fact] Gemini 3 Pro reportedly hit 1501 Elo on LMArena at launch,
  reported as the highest score recorded at the time.
- [fact] Claude Opus 4.5 was reported as the first model to score above
  80% on SWE-bench Verified.
- [fact] This window independently corroborates DHH's own dated claim
  (see `dhh_lexfridman501`) that Nov 24, 2025 was a personal "dividing
  line" moment: that's the exact date Opus 4.5 shipped.

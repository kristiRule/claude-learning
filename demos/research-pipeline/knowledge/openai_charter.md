## Claims

- [fact] OpenAI's charter defines AGI as "highly autonomous systems that
  outperform humans at most economically valuable work" — this is
  OpenAI's own stated working definition, not a field-wide consensus
  definition.

## Limitations

One-sentence definitional source — useful as a data point, not a full
picture. Must be paired with at least one other framing (DeepMind's
"Levels of AGI," or the lack-of-consensus point itself) so the talk
doesn't present OpenAI's definition as *the* definition.

## Relevance

Primary source for **Section 8 (AGI: what the term means)** — the
clearest single-sentence definition from a major lab, good as a contrast
point against DeepMind's more structured "levels" framing and against
the plain fact that no field-wide consensus definition exists.

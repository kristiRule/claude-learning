## Claims

- [fact] Chatbot Arena was created by researchers at UC Berkeley/LMSYS as
  a crowdsourced platform for evaluating LLMs via blind, randomized
  head-to-head human voting, using an Elo-style ranking system, based on
  over 300,000 user votes at time of reporting.
- [fact] The platform has since rebranded/moved to LMArena at
  https://lmarena.ai, distinct from the original chat.lmsys.org site —
  worth naming explicitly as an example of how fast even the field's own
  measurement infrastructure changes.
- [fact] Its differentiator vs. static benchmark datasets (MMLU, SWE-bench,
  etc.) is that it measures human preference on live, open-ended prompts
  rather than fixed test-set accuracy.

## Limitations

Human-preference voting has known biases (e.g., verbosity/formatting
preferences, voter self-selection) — worth a one-line caveat in the
benchmarks section rather than presenting Arena rank as a pure capability
measure.
